M04R research audit and preflight POC evidence

Evidence date: 24 August 2026. Scope: exact or progressive historical OHLCV analogue retrieval over the complete NASDAQ archive under the frozen distance-v1 score.

Purpose: resolve the high-risk design questions before another large implementation cycle. This report separates published claims, repository capabilities, measured local evidence and remaining proof obligations. It does not claim trading profitability.

Why the earlier plan reached this problem late

The original plans correctly covered causal OHLCV representation, benchmark context, exact reranking, exhaustive reference authorities, provenance, resource limits and outcome isolation. Gate 12 also explicitly warned that the 240-value signature was not a mathematical lower bound and required exact full-universe authorities.

The process gap was the missing contract between the approximate candidate representation and the exact scorer. Sampled-real gates contained only 2,658 NASDAQ candidates, while production contains roughly 2.6–3.8 million eligible episodes per query. Synthetic gates did not include four million distractors, a true neighbour below local rank 20, or separate-view decoy swarms. Deterministic local top-20 selection and equal reciprocal-rank fusion were tested for reproducibility, not for preservation of exact rank.

The first sealed full-universe M04 comparison therefore found what earlier documents had left open: historical TVTX retained 18/20 exact neighbours and historical CAKE retained 15/20. The exact scorer and authority remained correct; the shortlist was incomplete. A stricter process would have run a small authority-versus-proposal bakeoff before completing all 24 authorities and would have retired the whole original registry after the first development scout.

Research method and coverage

Three independent audits covered academic retrieval methods, open-source/production systems and the local architecture/history. Searches followed references through exact time-series indexes, elastic-distance lower bounds, multivariate and variable-length retrieval, motif/shape representations, financial-chart matching, progressive search, vector search, storage formats and evaluation leakage. Current project/repository documentation and licenses were inspected where available.

No finite search proves that every publication or private repository has been found. The control against endless rediscovery is a categorized evidence map, explicit rejection reasons, bounded POCs for material uncertainties and a frozen change process: new work enters only when it defeats a selected baseline under the same gate or a current gate fails.

Architecture frozen by the evidence

  1. Keep distance-v1 as the historical reference contract. Do not relabel another metric as the same truth.
  2. Build a packed immutable, query-independent, full float16 lower-bound store with masks and outward error radii. Quantization is part of the bound proof, never an unchecked approximation.
  3. Use a deterministic exact-aligned proposal to seed the initial top-k threshold; it may affect speed but never exclusion correctness.
  4. Scan every eligible lower-bound row in blocks. A row is either safely pruned or reconstructed and exact-scored. The certificate must close eligible = pruned + exact_evaluated.
  5. Finish survivors with compiled threshold-aware DTW and reconstruct full alignment paths only for final displayed matches.
  6. Expose interactive-progressive and exact-certified as distinct modes. Batch multiple news/current queries to amortize store I/O.
  7. Research a separately versioned fixed-normalization distance-v2 only after v1 is operational; it must win semantic/synthetic stability gates before gaining its own authorities.

Capacity consequence: the current store cannot be built safely until free capacity covers the 5 GiB reserve, active and shadow generations, metadata, 20% safety and atomic-write overhead. The final plan uses a 30 GiB free-space precondition unless a measured packed format lowers it.

Claims deliberately not made

  • Zero sampled violations are not a mathematical proof; the derivation needs independent review and exhaustive tiny-domain/property testing.
  • The 8.43 GiB projection excludes identifiers, masks, manifests and shadow-build overhead.
  • The Python scan rate is not the final packed/native rate and the warm compact benchmark is not a cold-disk measurement.
  • Exact retrieval under distance-v1 does not prove that the distance captures every professional trader judgement.
  • Neither similarity nor historical-neighbour outcomes prove a profitable trade. Outcome testing remains a later preregistered phase.

Change control that prevents another loop

  1. The companion implementation plan, distance contract, store schema and acceptance thresholds are frozen before the full build.
  2. One numbered task is implemented and verified at a time; a gate failure is preserved as evidence.
  3. A new paper/repository idea enters only through a bounded POC against the selected baseline with the same correctness/resource metrics.
  4. It replaces the baseline only if it passes correctness and materially improves a declared Pareto objective. Otherwise it is recorded and closed.
  5. Any inspected authority registry is development data forever. Final validation uses a newly frozen untouched registry once.
