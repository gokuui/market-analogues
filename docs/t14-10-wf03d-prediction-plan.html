WF-03D — Contract-conforming neighbours and causal predictions

Frozen boundary: retrieval outcomes are still unopened. The 2024–August-2025 final query outcomes remain single-open and sealed. This plan repairs a pre-outcome contract mismatch, constructs analogue evidence and freezes predictions; it cannot claim usefulness.

D0 — Audit and preserve the mismatch COMPLETE

The generic exact-search eligibility rule excludes the query episode and overlapping same-symbol windows, but permits older non-overlapping windows from that symbol. The walk-forward contract separately says same_query_symbol_excluded=true. Across 3,936 queries the retained stores contain 78 composite links in 78 queries, 94 price links in 94 queries, and 40 baseline links in 40 queries (16 random and 24 return/volatility). No result is relabelled or deleted.

The four stores contain 314,880 links and 274,343 unique matched episodes. The earlier T14-09 store contains outcomes for 56,378 episodes but overlaps this union on only 7,954; it is a verified reusable subset, not a complete walk-forward outcome store.

D1 — Exact query-symbol exclusion repair COMPLETE AND INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED

For an unaffected query, its certified top-20 over the original superset contains no excluded symbol and is therefore also the exact top-20 over the subset. Reuse it by reference and publish a subset-proof receipt.

For an affected query, retrieve the certified top-21 under the original one-result-per-symbol rule and remove the query-symbol row. Because at most one result can come from that symbol, the remaining first 20 are exactly the top-20 over the required subset. Composite and price repairs retain full search certificates; deterministic-random and return/volatility repairs are independently reconstructed from their exact orderings.

Pass: 3,936 queries × four methods, exactly 20 distinct non-query symbols per method, unchanged ranks for every unaffected query, exact top-21/drop proof for every affected query, and zero outcome or label access.

D2 — Cross-store manifest COMPLETE AND INDEPENDENTLY VERIFIED

Publish one immutable raw-link table with 314,880 rows, one source receipt binding per row and a deduplicated episode-request table. Verify exact query coverage, method/rank uniqueness, source identities, timestamps, causal gap, symbol exclusion and file-manifest closure.

D3 — Walk-forward analogue outcome store NEXT

Reuse exact rows from T14-09 only when episode identity, source fingerprint and outcome contract match. Compute missing episode outcomes with the already verified T14-09 formulas. Every measure carries its completion timestamp; a link contributes to a query only when that timestamp is no later than the query cutoff. Ambiguous, censored and truncated states remain explicit.

Pass: producer and independent oracle agree on identities, paths, outcomes, completion timestamps, reuse and causal eligibility; future mutation cannot change any earlier eligible row.

D4 — Prediction-before-outcome store

Freeze weighted and unweighted analogue probabilities, continuous quantiles, novelty, instability, effective counts and forced/selective lane fields. Process expanding market/regime baselines month by month: seal a month’s predictions first, then—and only then—open that month’s query outcomes to update later baseline state.

Pass: prediction mutation tests, independent formula reconstruction, restart-safe create-only receipts and a manifest proving that no query future affected its own prediction.

D5 — Non-final evaluation, then single-open final

Evaluate development and validation 1–3 under the frozen Brier/log/interval and dependence-aware inference contract. Only after those artifacts and final predictions independently verify may the 2024–August-2025 outcomes open once. Failure is reported without same-period tuning.
