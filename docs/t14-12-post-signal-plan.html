T14-12 — Does anything useful happen after the 4% signal?

Why this study is required: T14-11 verified that 4% days occur disproportionately inside 25% advances. Because the signal day was part of the winning window, that result cannot answer whether a trader benefits after observing the signal.

P0 — Freeze the clock

A signal is known only at its session close. It must follow 20 sessions without the same signal. The primary execution origin is the next exact NASDAQ session open; all future returns exclude the signal day. Future availability cannot determine whether a signal exists.

Gate: Synthetic future mutation changes outcomes but cannot change signal identity, at-risk status, investability, matching variables or market state.

P1 — Build the causal daily panel

Scan the same 12,080 fingerprint-locked histories with 12 restartable workers. Store all causally eligible at-risk rows, both signal definitions, pre-signal price/return/volatility/liquidity variables, contemporaneous benchmark state and censored 5/20/60-session post-signal outcomes.

Gate: Independent scalar reconstruction of start/middle/end rows across every shard, plus exact all-symbol counts and file seals.

P2 — Form signal episodes and controls

For each signal definition separately, select causal refractory events. Match five same-date non-signal stocks that were equally at risk, using only variables ending before the signal day. Broad and investable populations remain separate; every relaxation and shortfall is retained.

Gate: Reconstruct every decile, selection digest, rank, non-signal condition and future-independent match identity.

P3 — Measure genuinely subsequent behavior

From next-session open, calculate absolute and benchmark-relative log returns, endpoint +25% rates, MFE, MAE and 20-session ATR barrier routes. Five sessions are descriptive; 20 and 60 are primary.

Gate: Vector and independent scalar outcome oracles agree exactly on gaps, missing sessions, censoring, next-open entry and same-bar barrier ambiguity.

P4 — Dependence-aware inference

For benchmark-relative log return and endpoint +25% probability, compare every signal with the mean of its five controls. Aggregate paired differences by calendar month, run a three-month moving-block bootstrap and apply one Holm correction across 16 primary cells. Also require positive direction in at least four of five frozen eras.

Gate: A feature is authorized only when both primary metrics pass in broad and investable populations at the same horizon.

P5 — Integrate or reject

If the gate passes, add the signal as a causal event/context channel to analogue representation and freeze a new untouched evaluation. If it fails, retain the useful retrospective description but do not promote the signal into retrieval or ranking.
