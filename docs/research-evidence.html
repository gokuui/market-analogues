Research evidence map

This is a decision-oriented literature map, not a claim that one paper solves the problem. It records what was adopted, what remains experimental, and what was rejected for the first deterministic retrieval baseline.

Similarity and representation

EvidenceUseful resultDecision in this project
Zhao & Itti, shapeDTW (2018)Local shape descriptors can reduce raw-DTW’s pathological point-to-subsequence alignments; reported wins over DTW on 64 of 84 UCR datasets.ADOPTED IN PRINCIPLE: multichannel local chart state plus bounded warping. Full shapeDTW descriptor remains an ablation candidate.
SC-DTW (2015) and limited-path DTW (2017)Unconstrained DTW can create singularities; local context and path constraints reduce unintuitive alignments.ADOPTED: Sakoe–Chiba band, multivariate state, rigid and stage distances retained alongside DTW.
PR-DTW for stock series (2020)Directly studies morphological stock-series representation under time shifts and warpings.ABLATION: compare PR-DTW representation with dense-v1; do not replace volume/context channels based on close-only results.
Subsequence DTW for NYSE charting (2018)Evaluates generic chart-pattern matching with DTW, subsequence DTW, and derivative DTW rather than requiring named formations.ADOPTED: retrieval framing and DDTW/elastic baselines belong in evaluation; prediction claims are not imported.
Dynamic multi-perspective stock similarity (2021)Argues that stock similarity benefits from more than one perspective and handles time shifts through DTW.ADOPTED: component groups for price, candle/volatility, volume/shock, market context, and structure.
Lin et al., SAX (2007)PAA plus symbolic discretization enables compact words and indexing.DEFERRED: useful as an independent interpretable baseline, but early discretization risks losing magnitude/candle/volume detail.
Perceptually important points for stock matching (2007)Compresses a price path around visually important extrema before matching, closely reflecting how chart structure is perceived.ADOPTED IN SPIRIT: multi-threshold directional-change events preserve confirmed extrema; PIP remains an explicit structural ablation.

Motifs and scalable candidate generation

EvidenceUseful resultDecision
Matrix Profile variable-length motifs, STUMPY, and financial applicationsMatrix Profile/MASS provides exact normalized Euclidean subsequence search and mature motif/discord tooling.ADOPTED: optional STUMPY/MASS backend for long univariate coarse scans, differentially checked against the vector correlation implementation. Multichannel reranking remains local.
Lemire, two-pass LB_Keogh (2009) and Webb & Petitjean, tighter DTW bounds (2021)DTW candidates can be pruned without losing exact nearest neighbors.SCALE PHASE: coarse vector is implemented first; LB_Keogh/LB_Webb should be measured when full-universe exact reranking becomes the bottleneck.
UCR Time Series Archive, aeon, and tslearnStandardized 1-NN evaluations and independent reference implementations expose the effect of the distance itself.ADOPTED: GunPoint 1-NN benchmark plus differential DTW tests. It also exposed that the domain composite is not universally best.

Verification and financial validity

EvidenceUseful resultDecision
Murphy et al., metamorphic testing (2011) and METTLE (2018)When individual outputs lack a perfect oracle, known relations between transformed inputs and outputs can reveal defects and validate unsupervised systems.ADOPTED: price/volume scaling invariance, future mutation, clone recovery, positive/negative ordering, and context contradiction tests.
Shumway, delisting bias (1997)Missing negative delisting returns materially bias historical stock databases.REQUIRED WARNING: retrieval may operate on current archives, but any outcome claim must disclose universe/delisting coverage.
Carr & López de Prado, backtest overfitting (2014)Repeated strategy calibration against the same history contributes to selection and backtest overfitting.ADOPTED BOUNDARY: similarity verification is separate from trading performance; future strategy work needs a locked walk-forward protocol and trial accounting.

Resulting experiment matrix

  • Rigid dense-v1 vs bounded multivariate DTW vs stage/structural composite.
  • Close-only vs OHLC geometry vs +volume vs +benchmark/relative context.
  • Raw DTW vs derivative/shapeDTW vs constrained paths.
  • Coarse exact index vs approximate index vs Matrix Profile/LB pruning at scale.
  • Same-market vs explicit cross-market retrieval, reported separately.
  • Synthetic oracle first; then time-ordered real-data stability. Outcome evaluation never tunes representation on the same temporal fold.
