M04R — certified NASDAQ analogue retrieval remediation

Planning date: 24 August 2026. Status: final evidence-backed implementation plan, frozen after the research/POC preflight. M04R-00 through M04R-02 are verified complete; M04R-03 is next. No retrieval repair is claimed yet.

Target outcome: given a current 252-session NASDAQ chart state, return the historically eligible charts with the smallest frozen exact OHLCV/volume/market-context distance. Interactive mode must be fast and candid about approximation. Certified mode must prove that no eligible episode capable of beating the final constrained top-20 was discarded.

This plan is outcome-blind. Forward returns, trader profits and setup names are not inputs to representation, retrieval, tuning or acceptance.

Decision

Replace the fixed-pool correctness assumption with a deterministic certified filter–refine engine. An exact-aligned proposal supplies a good starting upper bound. A packed full float16 lower-bound representation, corrected by outward quantization-error radii, then accounts for every eligible episode. Native exact completion is performed only for candidates that remain capable of entering the top-20. Generic ANN may later seed the upper bound, but it can never decide that an episode is safe to exclude.

The current exact distance remains distance-v1 while its mathematical contract and selected quantized lower bounds receive independent proof review. If the selected store fails proof, capacity or packed-performance gates, a separately versioned and preregistered distance-v2 branch will use fixed causal scaling and a proof-friendly additive DTW contract. Gate 12 is never overwritten.

Research and POC preflight is complete

The detailed paper/repository audit, local-plan retrospective, POC methods, failures and measured results are in M04R research audit and preflight POC evidence. The preflight rejected fusion-only repair, compact PAA/moment authority layouts and DCT/SFA as the primary bound. It selected the full error-corrected float16 lower-bound row because it retained 99.83% of native pruning on the difficult CAKE sample while the compact candidates retained only 12–15%.

The selected lower-bound payload projects to 8.43 GiB for 3.82m NASDAQ rows before identifiers, masks and metadata. Current Python evidence is about 9,084 rows/second, so packed/native scan optimization is a mandatory gate rather than an assumed result. A full build is forbidden until the capacity and 1% packed-layout gates pass.

Target query architecture

1 · EligibilityDataset, quality, causal cutoff, history gap, same-symbol overlap and immutable data generation are applied before ranking.
2 · ProposalBlockwise exact-aligned signature scan proposes plausible episodes and exact-scores a small deterministic seed set to establish upper bound τ.
3 · Safe boundsEvery eligible episode receives a proven composite lower bound from full exact-aligned float16 fields. Quantization error is subtracted and denominators are bounded conservatively.
4 · Exact refineOnly unresolved episodes are reconstructed by instrument, then passed through tighter DTW bounds and threshold-aware exact completion.
5 · CertificateReturn the top-20 plus eligible/scored/pruned accounting, final τ, next bound, tie/overlap state and all source/contract digests.

Interactive mode may stop after a declared latency budget and reports that it is approximate. Certified mode finishes the bound scan and is the only mode permitted to claim complete top-20 retrieval.

Critical mathematical warning

Published DTW bounds cannot be copied mechanically. Current distance-v1 uses four dependent channels, query–candidate joint robust scaling, root-mean-square local cost, Sakoe–Chiba restriction and cumulative-cost division by the realized alignment length. Most literature assumes a fixed norm and unnormalized accumulated cost. Every new bound must have a written derivation for this exact formula and then pass adversarial property tests. If the derivation fails, it is proposal-only.

M04R-02 — Freeze and independently audit distance-v1

Build: Write a mathematical contract for all channel transformations, masks, pair scaling, weights, DTW local cost/band/path normalization, tie-breaking, temporal eligibility, overlap suppression and per-instrument constraints. Implement a slow independent reference, not a wrapper around production functions.

Tests: Identity/symmetry/triangle diagnostics; constants, zero volume, gaps, missing context, extreme finite values, ties and unequal alignment paths; scalar/batch component and path parity; future-data invariance.

Bound gate: Prove every proposed bound algebraically. Then require LB ≤ exact on at least one million random/adversarial pairs with zero violation. Tests support the proof; they do not replace it.

Branch rule: If v1 cannot produce useful bounds within the storage/runtime budget, preregister distance-v2 with fixed causal normalization and proof-friendly DTW, then rebuild authorities under a new name.

Verified 24 August 2026: PASS. A digest-bound contract freezes every component, mask, pair scale, weight, DTW band/local cost/path normalization/tie rule and retrieval constraint. A separate slow implementation matches production across synthetic, adversarial, sliding-batch and all 240 stored NASDAQ authority pairs: maximum total/component deltas are 4.44e-16/8.88e-16, with zero alignment, symmetry, identity or finite-output failures. The audit exposed float32 coarse arithmetic, which was promoted to float64 in scalar and batch paths while remaining within 1e-6 of stored authorities. The native symmetric LB_Keogh proof is frozen in contract 458e4280…575a6; a one-million-pair random/adversarial gate plus all 729 length-three ternary pairs found zero LB > exact + 1e-12 violations. Two real executions produced identical result digest 61a105f0…3be77 and machine SHA-256 6bb0aabc…66e86. The full quantized bound is explicitly deferred to M04R-04.

M04R-05 — Proposal signature v2 Pareto experiment

M04R-06 — Packed immutable store generations

Build: Replace 11,584 small-file opens with content-addressed packs containing fixed-width episode IDs, cutoff/session index, symbol dictionary ID, tier, masks, proposal fields and certified-bound fields. Activate a completed generation with one atomic manifest swap; daily updates append new immutable time segments.

Precondition: free ≥ 30 GiB, representing the 5 GiB reserve, active and shadow generations, metadata, 20% safety and atomic-write overhead. This may be lowered only by a passed measured packed-format projection; it may not be waived from an optimistic compression estimate.

Tests: Interrupted build/resume, truncated/corrupt/missing pack, manifest tamper, source-prefix revision, append-only incremental equivalence, serial/parallel digest equality and rollback.

Performance gate: On a 1% real packed store, project ≤300 seconds warm / 600 seconds cold for the full quantile-aware bound scan, ≤1 GiB RSS and ≤11 GiB active NASDAQ store including metadata. Compare raw segmented mmap, Blosc2 if available, and a compiled NumPy/Numba/NumKong-style kernel. Only then build 100%.

M04R-07 — Global streaming proposal selection

Build: Scan every eligible row in blocks and maintain bounded stable heaps. Remove the correctness-critical per-symbol top-20 truncation and the 560k–900k pandas materialization. Eligibility is applied before ranking; diversity is applied after exact scoring.

Routes: Exact-weighted composite plus reserved global quotas for stage, price, candle/volatility, volume/shock, market, structural and a small legacy/M03b topology fallback. A route-selected candidate cannot be discarded by later fusion.

Adversaries: True neighbour at local rank 28, 25 same-symbol decoys, different decoys winning each view, poor equal-RRF rank, ties, shuffled packs, overlapping near-clones and future near-clones.

Pass before next: All planted neighbours survive; zero duplicates/temporal violations; exact row accounting; IDs invariant to block/order/worker count; ≤768 MiB proposal RSS.

M04R-08 — Certified scan and grouped exact completion

Upper bound: Exact-score a small deterministic proposal seed to establish τ. A poor or empty proposal may slow the query but may never change the answer.

Safe cascade: Metadata → cheap stage/structure/coarse partial sum → full quantile-aware error-corrected non-DTW bound → optional proved multivariate envelope bound → compiled threshold-aware DTW. Order fields by measured pruning benefit per bytes and CPU while preserving the identical total bound.

Materialization: Group unresolved cutoffs by instrument, load each source once, and choose adaptively between sparse requested-window reconstruction and full sliding batch construction. Reconstruct DTW paths only for final results.

Batch mode: Accept multiple current/news queries, read each packed block once, maintain a separate τ/certificate state per query and report per-query plus shared I/O time. One difficult query may not weaken another query's certificate.

Certificate: Eligible = safely pruned + exact evaluated; final constrained top-k full; minimum unvisited bound strictly above the kth distance or literal exhaustion; stable episode-ID tie rule; all input/store/distance digests; per-stage prune counts.

Pass before next: Exact IDs/order/totals/components/alignments equal brute force on small/medium universes and every existing authority; repeated certificate digest identical; single-query p95 ≤300 seconds, maximum ≤600 seconds, RSS ≤1,536 MiB; 24-query batch must materially reduce total elapsed time versus 24 independent scans without changing any result.

M04R-09 — Development bake-off and optional ANN

Compare: No proposer, proposal-v2 flat scan, weighted route union, Faiss flat/HNSW and USearch mmap/custom or aligned-L2 proposal. All share the identical certified fallback.

Judge: End-to-end p50/p95/max latency, bytes read, RSS/disk, exact completions, determinism and worst-case query—not ANN recall alone.

Acceptance: ANN is retained only if it materially lowers certified latency without changing one final result or certificate and without violating disk/RSS/reproducibility constraints. Otherwise deterministic scan remains the default.

M04R-11 — Build truth first, then run the untouched matrix once

Authority phase: Build every exact certified authority and aggregate it before any proposal-v2 result is opened. Interrupted work resumes through validated shards; failures remain visible.

Single validation: Run proposal and certified modes once against the frozen matrix.

Fast-mode pass: ≥19/20 exact-authority inclusion in every case, target 20/20; mean ≥99%; zero temporal/overlap/tier/duplicate errors; repeated search digest; ≤60 seconds warm / 120 seconds cold and ≤1 GiB RSS.

Certified-mode pass: 20/20 exact IDs and order by construction in every case, complete certificate accounting, p95 ≤300 seconds, max ≤600 seconds, RSS ≤1,536 MiB. One failed case fails the entire matrix; an average cannot override it.

M04R-12 — Fault injection, shadow operation and promotion

Faults: Equivalent rewrite, future append, historical revision, partial session, timezone/calendar shift, split/scale transform, missing benchmark, sparse/zero volume, delisted/renamed aliases, tie at kth distance, corrupt pack, disk full, interrupted build, worker crash and stale manifest.

Shadow: Run daily NASDAQ queries without trading decisions. Compare interactive/certified route agreement, repeated-query stability, incremental versus clean-build digest, store coverage and drift telemetry.

Output contract: Every result labels its mode; displays query/store coverage, final exact neighbours and components, exclusions/certificate, known data limitations and abstention reason when provenance or coverage is incomplete.

Promotion: Default interactive mode only after M04R-11 and shadow gates pass. Certified mode remains available for audit and hard queries. Profitability/outcome evaluation is a later, separately preregistered phase.

  5. Commit a clean rollback point.
  6. Proceed only if the explicit gate passes. A failure creates a versioned redesign; it never lowers a frozen threshold.
