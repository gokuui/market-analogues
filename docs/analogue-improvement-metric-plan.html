Analogue improvement metric and acceptance plan

Purpose: define the one gate a new analogue method must pass before anyone may say it improves decision evidence. The test is an untouched, causal, rolling walk-forward comparison. It rewards accurate probabilities, not attractive charts, in-sample fit, neighbour similarity, or a profitable-looking hand-picked example.

Existing result that must not be erased: the locked composite produced −3.22% Brier skill versus the unconditional base rate on pooled validation and −3.46% on the consumed final block. Those periods are now development evidence; neither may be called untouched again or used to certify a redesign.

1. What is—and is not—being required

The candidate remains a case-based market-memory system: select causally eligible historical analogues, then form a nonparametric outcome distribution from those analogues. A direct weighted empirical distribution is sufficient. No neural network, regression, classifier, or other fitted ML model is required.

For example, after outcome-blind retrieval and weighting, a three-class forecast may be formed as:

p̂ic = (½ + Σj∈N(i) wij · 1[yj=c]) / (3/2 + Σj∈N(i) wij)

The neighbour set N(i) and weights wij must depend only on information available at query cutoff i. Historical outcomes may summarize already-selected neighbours; they may not choose the representation, neighbours, distance weights, or stopping rule during the untouched evaluation.

3. The two mandatory comparators

A. Matched causal base rate

At each query cutoff, estimate class frequencies only from earlier registry queries whose complete 20-session outcomes were observable before that cutoff. Match using frozen, cutoff-known strata: market regime × prefix-quality class × trailing-liquidity cell. Use the exact stratum when it has at least 30 resolved observations; otherwise follow the preregistered fallback regime × liquidity → regime → unconditional. Apply the same half-count smoothing as the candidate. Bin definitions, support threshold, fallback order, and warm-up period freeze before outcomes.

This is the “what usually happens in comparable causal conditions?” baseline. It prevents a retriever from receiving credit merely for rediscovering the prevailing market frequency.

B. Current locked retriever

Run the presently locked composite retriever and probability construction unchanged on the same query rows, source snapshot, eligible historical information, outcome definition, and scoring code. Bind its implementation commit, configuration, representation digest, and runtime-file hashes in the preregistration. It is the incumbent—not a reimplemented approximation.

Common-support rule: all primary comparisons use exactly the rows on which the candidate, matched base rate, incumbent, and label are present. Coverage outside that intersection remains visible and is separately gated.

4. Primary score and improvement formula

For query i and classes c ∈ {favorable first, adverse first, no touch}, the multiclass Brier loss is:

BSi(m) = Σc=1..3 (p̂ic(m) − 1[yi=c])²

BS(m) = (1/N) Σi=1..N BSi(m)

BrierSkill(candidate, baseline) = 1 − BS(candidate) / BS(baseline)

Δi,b = BSi(candidate) − BSi(baseline)

Positive skill means lower loss. The candidate must have strictly positive Brier skill against both mandatory comparators. The paired statistical test is expressed on Δ: improvement means E[Δ] < 0.

Scores are first computed per query. For inference, paired loss differences are averaged within calendar month so correlated stocks in one market episode are not treated as independent trials.

5. Confidence and paired-test gate

  1. Use the same query/month pairs for candidate and comparator; never compare means from different coverage sets.
  2. The primary inference unit is the calendar month. Use a preregistered, seeded moving-block bootstrap of monthly mean paired loss differences with 10,000 replicates and fixed six-month blocks. Disclose three- and 12-month block sensitivities.
  3. For each mandatory comparator, test one-sided H0: E[Δ] ≥ 0 versus H1: E[Δ] < 0. Apply Holm correction across the two primary comparisons.
  4. Pass only if both Holm-adjusted p-values are below 0.05 and both simultaneous 95% upper confidence bounds for mean Δ are below zero.
  5. Report the effect size as Brier skill with confidence intervals, mean paired loss reduction, fraction of months improved, and fraction of queries improved. Statistical significance without positive practical effect does not pass.

A Newey–West/Diebold–Mariano calculation on monthly differences is a confirmation diagnostic. It cannot override a failed primary bootstrap gate.

6. Rolling walk-forward and untouched-data discipline

  1. Develop: use only previously opened WF-04 data and synthetic fixtures to choose the candidate. Preserve the old negative result.
  2. Preregister: freeze the candidate, both comparators, query registry, exact chronological folds, targets, exclusions, score code, random seeds, minimum coverage, tests, and performance budgets before opening any new query outcome.
  3. Predict: at each monthly cutoff, retrieve only episodes already observable at that time. A neighbour outcome contributes only if its full relevant horizon completed before the query cutoff; early barrier touch never shortens the embargo.
  4. Seal then reveal: atomically seal all candidate and baseline predictions, eligibility rows, provenance, and digests for a month before computing that month’s future labels.
  5. Expand: later months may use earlier months only after their outcomes mature. They may not revise any earlier prediction.
  6. Evaluate once: open the frozen untouched folds once and publish pass or failure. A failed method may inspire a new version, but needs a new future authority to make a new untouched claim.

Use at least four preregistered chronological folds. Fold boundaries must be calendar-derived without inspecting performance. Purge boundaries by the maximum disclosed 60-session horizon so no label can cross from a training/available-outcome interval into an evaluation interval.

9. Continuous-return evidence: CRPS is secondary

For each horizon separately, construct a causal predictive CDF Fi,h of stock close return and benchmark-relative return from the weighted analogue outcomes. Score it with the continuous ranked probability score:

CRPS(Fi,h, yi,h) = ∫−∞∞ (Fi,h(z) − 1[yi,h ≤ z])² dz

For an empirical sample, use the exact weighted discrete CRPS implementation frozen and verified on synthetic cases. Compare paired CRPS with both mandatory comparator distributions on common rows, using the same month-level dependence treatment. Also disclose median and 10/25/75/90% quantiles, interval width, empirical coverage, and benchmark-relative results.

Role: CRPS tests whether the analogue outcome distribution is useful beyond the categorical barrier target. It is supportive evidence only; superior CRPS cannot rescue a failed primary 20-session Brier gate.

10. Required artifacts and decision language

  • Outcome-blind preregistration with Git commit, source/config/runtime hashes, exact fold dates, sample-size gate, algorithm, baselines, metrics, seeds, and budgets.
  • Create-only monthly prediction receipts proving prediction-before-outcome order, plus complete neighbour/eligibility and fallback provenance.
  • Outcome store with explicit complete, ambiguous, censored, delisted/unavailable, and benchmark-missing accounting.
  • Per-query score table; monthly paired-difference table; fold/horizon/calibration/coverage/performance reports; immutable result digest.
  • Independent verifier that recomputes causal eligibility, predictions, labels, scores, inference, guardrails, and digests without calling producer scientific-validation code.

If every gate passes: “The frozen nonparametric analogue outcome method improved untouched 20-session probabilistic forecasts over both the matched causal base rate and the prior locked retriever in this research archive.”

If any gate fails: publish the failure and retain the method as descriptive research only. Do not claim calibrated predictive value, trading profitability, production readiness, or a buy/sell signal.

11. Implementation sequence

  1. M0: write the machine-readable metric contract and synthetic oracle; independently verify formulas, folds, embargo, coverage, CRPS, paired inference, and failure paths.
  2. M1: freeze one candidate and the two comparator implementations; produce a source/runtime/authority manifest.
  3. M2: build the untouched outcome-blind rolling query registry and prediction listener with restart-safe, create-only monthly seals.
  4. M3: run and seal monthly predictions; reveal outcomes only when all horizons mature.
  5. M4: execute the single-open evaluation and independent verification.
  6. M5: issue a pass/fail receipt using only the decision language above and append the immutable result to the running development log.
