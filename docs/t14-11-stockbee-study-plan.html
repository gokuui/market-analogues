T14-11 — Stockbee 25% move / 4% expansion replication

Question: Across the locked NASDAQ archive, how commonly do 25% one-month or one-quarter advances begin with one or more 4% moves—and is that exposure materially more common than in comparable non-winners?

S0 — Freeze semantics

Use 21- and 63-session endpoint returns of at least 25%. Treat a +4% close move, a 4% true-range day and a bullish range-expansion day as distinct. Measure the start day, first five sessions, full move and a pre-start sensitivity window.

S1 — Verify the universe

Bind 12,080 fingerprint-locked histories and the NASDAQ benchmark. Validate ordering, OHLC, volume, adjusted-price continuity and the March 2026 cutoff. Preserve inactive histories and state the missing point-in-time membership/delisting limitation.

S2 — Build a restartable risk set

Use 12 one-thread worker processes and atomic symbol-hash shards. For every causally eligible symbol-session, compute future winner labels plus only trailing/start-day exposures and matching covariates. Synthetic arrays must verify every offset, horizon and event-clustering boundary first.

S3 — Cluster events

Report both all qualifying starts and one representative per maximal consecutive qualifying run, retaining run length and peak return. This prevents overlapping rolling windows from pretending one sustained move is dozens of independent discoveries.

S4 — Construct controls

Compare winners with the entire same-horizon non-winner risk set and five deterministic same-date controls matched on trailing return, volatility, price and dollar-volume deciles. Retain matching relaxation and shortfalls.

S5 — Estimate and infer

Report prevalence, risk difference, risk ratio, odds ratio and predictive lift. Use calendar-month block inference and Holm correction. A high winner prevalence is not enough: the exposure must exceed controls, have adequate coverage and agree in broad and investable populations.

S6 — Independent verification

Recompute a frozen cross-horizon sample from raw OHLCV, validate every shard and digest, reconstruct events and controls, and reproduce aggregate statistics. Publish the complete HTML report whether the quote is supported, weakened or rejected.
