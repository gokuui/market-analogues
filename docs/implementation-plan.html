Historical Market Analogue Engine

Question answered: “Among all historical stock paths known before today, which episodes most closely resemble this stock’s present development, and what happened after those episodes?”

This is retrieval and evidence display—not a named-pattern classifier, return predictor, or trading signal.

System boundary

InputAny daily OHLCV directory or long table in CSV/Parquet, with an optional benchmark. Dataset adapters map source columns into one canonical schema.
RepresentationCausal price path, returns/gaps/intraday moves, candle geometry, volatility/ATR, robust volume and shocks, contraction, distances from highs/averages, benchmark regime, relative strength, multi-scale directional events.
RetrievalEither a bounded-memory streaming correlation/magnitude scan (optional STUMPY/MASS backend) or a portable 128-dimensional persisted index, then exact explainable reranking with rigid, stage-wise, bounded-DTW, context, volume, volatility, and structural distances.
OutputDeduplicated historical episodes, component-by-component reasons, quality warnings, and separately computed forward-outcome distributions including quartiles.
Explicit exclusionsNo learned embedding; no supervised return model; no use of future bars in representation or ranking; no assumption that NASDAQ and NSE share file conventions.

Completed gated tasks

01 — Adapter and data-quality contract

Implemented directory and long-table adapters, canonical ordering/types, source SHA-256 fingerprints, benchmark isolation, and A/B/QUARANTINED quality tiers. Source paths are read-only.

Gate: schema, sorting, determinism, missing/invalid rows, duplicate timestamp, discontinuity, and quarantine tests.

02 — Causal episodes and complete chart state

Implemented 21/63/126/252-bar episodes at configurable stride. Benchmark joins are exact-timestamp; missing sessions remain unknown. Dense features retain direction, magnitude, candle anatomy, volume, shocks, compression, relative strength, and regime.

Gate: price/volume-unit invariance and future-mutation tests must be exact.

03 — Structural and elastic views

Implemented causally confirmed directional-change events at 3%, 6%, and 12%, stage signatures, and Sakoe–Chiba-banded multivariate DTW. No event may be dated at a future extreme.

Gate: injected peak/valley confirmation and prefix-stability tests.

04 — Synthetic verification oracle

Five deterministic latent families generate valid OHLCV plus market context. Metamorphic positives change price/volume units or add small perturbations. Critical negatives reverse paths or contradict market context.

Gate thresholds: clone rank-1 100%; hard invariance 100%; pair ordering ≥95%; nDCG@10 ≥0.90; family recall@10 ≥0.80; critical-negative error ≤2%; cross-dataset degradation ≤5 points.

05 — Candidate generation and exact reranking

A persisted coarse index or streaming scan reduces the candidate pool. The streaming path scans each symbol without materializing every episode. Exact component distance remains the authority, provides a transparent breakdown, enforces a 60-session history gap, and removes overlapping duplicates.

Gate: vector scan must recover an inserted historical path exactly; STUMPY/MASS and vector rankings must agree in differential tests; stored coarse-index round-trip must not change neighbors.

06 — Outcomes and reporting

Forward return, maximum favorable excursion, and maximum adverse excursion are computed only after matches exist. Incomplete horizons are censored. HTML reports label the result descriptive rather than predictive and carry provenance.

Gate: cutoff arithmetic, censoring, sample-size accounting, and self-contained report tests.

07 — Complete-universe coverage and performance

The same engine covered all 1,843 quality-eligible NSE instruments (1,040,155 windows) in 92.61 seconds and all 11,584 eligible NASDAQ instruments (3,786,156 windows) in 203.49 seconds. Peak RSS remained below 454 MB and no eligible instrument failed.

08 — Prior-art comparison

Public stock matchers, Matrix Profile/MASS, motif benchmarks, ShapeDTW, aeon, and tslearn were inspected or run through a common interface. Their best ideas were incorporated as streaming candidate generation, optional MASS acceleration, overlap control, outcome quartiles, and differential baselines.

Gate: every method runs on the same deterministic financial corpus; generic shape behavior is checked independently on UCR GunPoint.

09–10 — Sampled candidate fidelity — PASS; complete-universe fidelity — OPEN

The deterministic exact oracle now covers 24 cases and 5,850 episodes across both markets, three lookbacks, historical/current cutoffs, quality tiers, and liquidity strata. The cheap seven-view union reaches a worst-case 0.95 top-20 recall at pool 175 on those locked sampled cases without reducing the synthetic verifier.

The independent full-universe smoke queries do not saturate: pool-175 recall versus pool 1,000 is 0.40 NSE and 0.50 NASDAQ, and raw-on-query multiview latency misses the production budget. Pool 1,000 is not exhaustive. Therefore the system remains retrieval-incomplete at exchange scale. See the current evidence and exact-safe next plan.

Honest limitations and next scale gate

  • The available NSE archive contains known split/adjustment concerns; the available NASDAQ archive contains discontinuity and stale/zero-volume histories. Quality tiers expose these issues but cannot reconstruct missing corporate actions.
  • Complete-universe latency and coverage now pass, but candidate fidelity does not. A larger candidate pool improves results without yet demonstrating exhaustive nearest-neighbor recall.
  • Historical neighbors do not establish causal predictability. Outcome distributions need walk-forward calibration, delisting-aware data, multiple-testing controls, and regime-stratified stability checks before any trading use.
