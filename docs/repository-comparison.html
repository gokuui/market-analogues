Repository comparison and adopted ideas

Audit snapshot: 18 August 2026. The objective was not to copy a stock-pattern repository; it was to identify proven candidate-generation, distance, deduplication, and evaluation approaches, then test them under one causal OHLCV contract.

Measured result, not intuition

On 13 financial synthetic queries, the local multichannel composite reached 0.954 family recall@10 and 0.936 nDCG@10, versus 0.846/0.795 for correlation/MASS. On 40 UCR GunPoint test cases, Euclidean and correlation reached 0.900 1-NN accuracy, while the composite reached 0.800. This supports a two-stage system: fast generic shape retrieval followed by domain-rich reranking. It does not support a claim that the composite is universally superior.

Tangled and search limitations

GitHub repositories were inspected directly. Searches of Tangled’s publicly indexed surface did not identify a directly relevant OHLCV/DTW analogue repository. That is a search result, not proof that no private, unindexed, or differently named Tangled project exists. No source was copied from repositories without an identified license; concepts were reimplemented against this project’s own contracts and tests.
