Gate 12 — exhaustive reference and independent validation

Locked 19 August 2026, before measuring any Gate 12 recall. This plan answers the remaining correctness question: “Does the fast persisted pool contain the true constrained top-20 from every eligible historical window?” It does not treat pool 1,000, a signature ranking, or another approximation as truth.

Current boundary: Gate 11 proves sampled-oracle fidelity and a deterministic, fresh-data-validated 252-bar production path. It does not prove complete-exchange nearest neighbours. Gate 12 remains synthetic and machine-verifiable; no human chart judgement is an acceptance input.

Why another reference engine is required

The persisted 240-value signature is intentionally a candidate representation. Its view distances are not mathematical lower bounds on the exact composite, so an omitted window cannot be dismissed safely from signature rank alone. Gate 08 already showed that larger approximate pools continue to change results.

The exact representation contains 1,353 numeric values per episode: 128 coarse values, 19×48 channel samples, 4×64 DTW samples, 48 stage values and 9 structural values, plus missing-channel state. The original 26.4 GB float32 and 13.2 GB float16 figures were lower-precision planning estimates, not lossless projections. T12-01 measured the actual mixed-precision contract and found that only the 128 coarse values are float32; the remaining 1,225 exact values are float64. Therefore a blind full exact-feature store is prohibited, and the measured decision below supersedes those preliminary estimates.

The planned authority is instead a two-pass, memory-bounded search: compute the exact non-DTW lower bound for every eligible episode, persist only a compact sortable frontier, then reconstruct and finish DTW in global lower-bound order until the corrected constrained stopping proof is satisfied.

T12-01 — Exact-feature storage and compression feasibility spike — PASS

Build: On deterministic A/B and liquidity-stratified shards, serialize only the arrays required by representation_distance_lower_bound and DTW completion. Compare float32, lossless compressed float32 and any proposed derived/minimal layout. Float16 may be measured but cannot become the authority unless every oracle total/component remains within 1e-12 and ordering/ties remain identical.

Tests: deserialize-to-online parity for every representation field; lower bound and completed distance parity; missing benchmark, constant price, zero volume and extreme finite-value fixtures; corruption and version mismatch fail.

Pass before proceeding: projected full footprint plus temporary frontier leaves ≥5 GB free. If it fails—as the 26.4/13.2 GB raw estimates suggest—record the failure and select the streaming two-pass design without building the full artifact.

Evidence and decision: A 48-representation stratified probe measured all 1,353 values, optional-presence masks, serialized round trips, exact lower bounds, rigid-price terms, completed components/totals and rankings. Native float32/float64 has zero field or distance delta but projects to 52.81 GiB after the 1.25 compression safety factor; the frontier adds 0.29 GiB. Float32 projects to 27.12 GiB but changes totals by up to 6.81e-8; float16 projects to 13.95 GiB but changes totals by up to 4.06e-4. Both violate 1e-12. With 9.48 GiB free and a 5 GiB reserve, the selected authority is two_pass_streaming. Payload corruption/version mismatch and missing/constant/extreme fixtures pass their guards. Gate 12B passed; no full store was built.

Post-gate precision review: The 1e-12 rule is retained only for native implementation parity, not as a trading requirement. A later behavioral audit quantized all 5,850 Gate 09 candidates to float16: all 24 top-20 sets were identical, minimum graded nDCG was 0.9999925 and one case swapped ranks 16/17. Float16 is therefore approved for approximate candidate/cache layers followed by native reranking. It still cannot be the native stopping authority, and the projected store still exceeds available disk.

Performance gate: ≥20× scalar throughput on a stratified sample, ≤512 MB per worker, and no non-finite or silently skipped eligible window.

Evidence: Scalar differential across all 12 frozen symbols and 1,200 252-bar windows gives maximum total delta 8.88e-16 and component delta 3.55e-15. Every symbol clears 20× after a warm-up plus median-of-three batch protocol: corrected final minimum 21.1707×, aggregate 26.2109×, peak RSS 341.33 MB. Full/partial/missing context—including 63-bar entirely missing benchmark context—constant/zero-volume, future mutation and chunk-size identity tests pass. The first cold single-timing run failed three symbols and remains in gate history.

Execution discipline

  1. Implement one task only.
  2. Run its unit, adversarial, differential, determinism and resource checks.
  3. Write a machine gate and readable HTML evidence.
  4. Append what/why/how/failures/metrics to the development log.
  5. Commit a clean rollback point.
  6. Proceed only when the task’s explicit pass condition holds; a failure creates a recorded redesign task, not a threshold waiver.

Forward outcomes remain outside every retrieval and acceptance computation. Gate 12 validates historical similarity retrieval, not profitability or a trading forecast.
