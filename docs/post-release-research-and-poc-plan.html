Post-release research and POC plan

Executive decision

The released system already answers the mechanical question correctly: retrieve causally valid historical OHLCV/market-context analogues, freeze their ranking, and then report what happened next. E2E-01–05 pass; the final snapshot records 1,226 passing tests, 37 parsed HTML documents, clean Git integrity, restart/concurrency identity and independently reconstructed real-NSE evidence.1

The next problem is not another speed rewrite. It is whether the returned neighborhood is meaningful enough for a trader: is there a strong precedent or only a least-bad match, do a few generic episodes become hubs, is the match stable across chart views, are price/volume/market moves allowed realistic timing offsets, and did the historical futures form distinct failure/continuation modes?

Selected order: adequacy diagnostics → fixed-neighbor outcome modes → variable-duration/channel-lag POC → stronger synthetic coverage → optional alignment/structure explanations. No new distance replaces distance-v1 unless it wins a frozen, outcome-blind gate and receives its own exhaustive authority.

Hard boundaries

  • Retrieval correctness and predictive usefulness are separate. The existing walk-forward composite and 4% post-signal study did not authorize a predictive feature. That negative evidence remains.
  • No outcome-guided distance tuning. Forward returns cannot choose windows, channels, weights, lag limits, temperatures or normalization.
  • No forced confidence percentage. “75% similar” has no meaning until calibrated against causal null neighborhoods and stability. The output may say that no adequate precedent exists.
  • No silent adaptive channel choice. A query-dependent view may be shown as a separate panel, but it cannot quietly redefine the official composite.
  • No ML-model workstream. Learned embeddings remain deferred; deterministic representations and transparent retrieval are the active scope.
  • Current evidence is local. Large artifacts and source OHLCV live under Git-ignored paths and are not present in a source-only clone.

What the earlier research already covered

The existing evidence map already addresses ordinary, constrained, derivative and shape DTW; PAA, SAX, SFA and Haar summaries; Matrix Profile/MASS; perceptually important points and directional changes; MPdist, TWED and ERP; exact lower bounds; variable-length indexes; ANN proposal systems; metamorphic testing; delisting bias and backtest overfitting.2 The M04R audit also measured and rejected several compact bounds instead of assuming they would work. Re-running those same ideas is low value.

The new audit therefore looked specifically for gaps in multichannel timing, pattern maturity, neighborhood trust, future-path presentation, evolving motifs and explanation. No inspected open-source project combines the local system's causal source locks, custom exact score, full-universe certificate, overlap/diversity rules, restart integrity and post-retrieval outcome embargo.

R1-A result — causal exposure and concentration

Verified diagnostic: the outcome-blind audit covers all 3,270 sealed NASDAQ queries, 65,400 selected links, 3,786,156 candidate episodes, 11,584 candidate symbols and 106 distinct eligibility cutoffs. The producer passed all ten gates, its deterministic replay passed all eleven gates, and a separate post-publication verifier reconstructed the complete preregistration/result chain through 22 integrity gates. Result b22ec5f4…a4e2a; replay e31c31b6…a4df; full integrity reconstruction 36d80c00…f3c1; local report.

The frozen null assigns independent continuous random priorities within each query's exact causal A/B-tier risk set and then applies the unchanged production selector: at most three episodes per symbol, inclusive same-symbol overlap rejection and top-20 stopping. Across 512 preregistered replicates, the observed retrieval is more concentrated than every replicate on every registered metric (one-sided Monte Carlo floor 1/513). Episode maximum reuse is 38 versus null median 3 and p99 4; symbol maximum reuse is 90 versus null median 37 and p99 44; repeated-symbol queries are 13.76% versus null median 3.21%; mean cross-query episode overlap is 0.00408 versus null median 0.000106.

Interpretation boundary: this diagnostic shows that distance-ranked retrieval has substantially more shared structure than an independent random-priority causal baseline. It does not establish harmful hubness or useful precedent: recurring market states, genuine archetypes, shared query structure and metric domination remain competing explanations. The top-1% statistic uses one percent of the full declared candidate population, including zero-selection entities. No adequacy label, ranking change, predictive claim or production promotion is authorized. R1-B must use matched/shared-structure nulls and stability diagnostics before calibration.

Execution plan and gates

R1 — Analogue adequacy certificate

Goal: decide whether the top-20 is a strong neighborhood, a fragile neighborhood, or merely the nearest available set. Retrieval remains byte-identical.

  1. OPEN: freeze a development registry disjoint from all final evaluation cases.
  2. R1-A COMPLETE: compute exact causal eligibility exposure and compare reverse-neighbor concentration with an outcome-blind random-priority null. This is a structural diagnostic, not yet an adequacy label.
  3. R1-B: measure local distance contrast, rank-20/rank-1 ratio, symmetric offline pair geometry, and neighbor-set stability under one-group removal, small input perturbations and nearby cutoff shifts. Causal mutual-neighbor reciprocity is structurally impossible for distinct time-ordered episodes and will not be reported as a failure.
  4. R1-B: construct causal null queries matched on cutoff, history length, tier, liquidity and volatility, plus a shared-structure sensitivity null. Report each real query's distance percentile against those nulls.
  5. R1-C: publish three labels only after synthetic calibration: strong precedent, weak/unstable precedent, or insufficient evidence. Thresholds are frozen from synthetic/development data, never future returns.

Pass: clone/known-positive adequacy ≥95%; known negatives/novel paths abstain ≥95%; future mutation changes no label; exposure-normalized hub diagnostics reproduce independently; original neighbor identities remain unchanged. If calibration fails, publish raw diagnostics only.

R2 — Fixed-neighbor future-path modes

Goal: make “what happened next?” more useful without changing which histories were retrieved.

  1. Freeze the existing top-20 and their outcome eligibility before clustering.
  2. Normalize each observable 60/126-session future path at the historical cutoff. Keep absolute and benchmark-relative paths separate.
  3. Compare deterministic medoid clustering for k=1..4; select only through a frozen stability/separation rule. Never assign trader pattern names automatically.
  4. Show actual medoids, membership, frequency, median/IQR envelopes, MFE/MAE/barriers and censored counts. Store a cohort handle so filters can be replayed exactly.
  5. Mutate outcomes and prove retrieval digests and neighbors remain identical.

Pass: deterministic repeat identity; bootstrap adjusted-Rand stability ≥0.80; every displayed mode has ≥3 effective cases; all censored paths explicit; cluster count becomes one or abstains when modes are unstable. This is descriptive, not predictive.

R3 — Variable-duration and channel-lag distance-v2 POC

Goal: test how a professional reader tolerates different setup duration and volume/market lead-lag without discarding contradictions.

  1. Implement experiment-only variants: shared-path DTW; independent per-channel DTW; shared price path plus frozen ±1/±3/±5-session volume/context lags; causal boundary proposals with 63/126/189/252 lengths; and prefix-relaxed matching with a trim penalty.
  2. Generate synchronized positives, known volume leads, reversed/shifted volume decoys, benchmark contradictions, isolated shocks, irrelevant prefixes, partial maturity and tiny-fragment decoys.
  3. Compare every variant against distance-v1 on identical cases and compute a small exhaustive authority for each candidate metric.

Pass: 100% clone recovery; ≥98% positive-over-decoy ordering; ≤2% context/critical-negative errors; lag recovery within one session; no systematic minimum-length collapse; family nDCG improvement with a predeclared margin; acceptable latency/RSS. Otherwise retain v1.

R4 — Verifier-envelope expansion

Measure the causal real-prefix envelope over trend, ACF, spectral entropy, lumpiness, spikiness, volatility, nonlinearity and break strength. Use GRATIS-like generators to fill sparse cells, then synthesize valid OHLCV and benchmark coupling with known transformations. Report failures per cell, not only averages. Add million-distractor and fragment-decoy swarms before any v2 promotion.

R5 — Optional final-pool and explanation POCs

  • Soft-DTW divergence (not biased raw soft-DTW) and alignment entropy on only the exact top 128–256.
  • Actual medoid plus DBA/TEKA envelope, suppressed on leave-one-out instability.
  • CID, visibility-graph and recurrence diagnostics against coil-versus-chop negatives.
  • Fixed wavelet scattering and path-signature distances as transparent, no-training views.
  • Shapelet/Contrast-Profile highlights explaining supportive and contradictory local fragments. Outcome-derived fragments stay within each historical training fold.
  • STUMPY snippets/chains/multidimensional profiles for difficult-query discovery and developmental explanations.

R6 — Promotion and outcome test

Only a representation that passes R3/R4 may receive a versioned packed store and new untouched authority registry. Then run a preregistered expanding-window comparison against unconditional, recent-return/volatility, price-only and current-composite baselines. Promotion requires both retrieval semantics and out-of-sample Brier/log-loss improvement across populations/eras; persuasive charts alone are insufficient.

Stop rules

  • One numbered POC at a time; code, thresholds and evidence schema committed before opening real evaluation results.
  • Failed outputs remain immutable. A correction uses a new namespace and explains exactly why the earlier result failed.
  • A method is rejected when it misses any correctness boundary even if it is faster or visually attractive.
  • A method is closed as “no material improvement” when the declared semantic gain is absent; it is not repeatedly tuned on the same holdout.
  • No full-universe rebuild until a small exhaustive POC wins and an independent verifier reconstructs it.
