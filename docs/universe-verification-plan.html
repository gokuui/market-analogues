Interpretation

The engine can scan both complete universes quickly and reproducibly, but the cheap close-path ordering is weakly aligned with the rich multichannel exact distance. Expanding the candidate pool keeps introducing new top-20 exact matches. Therefore a small pool cannot be presented as “the nearest episodes in the universe.” The production default is raised to 1,000 as a safer temporary setting, but 1,000 is not claimed to be exhaustive.

Several attempted remedies were measured and rejected: simple reweighting reduced the financial synthetic oracle; a single weighted multiview proxy reduced saturation; and a vectorized dense proxy produced worse coarse/exact correlation. The stable metric and its 0.954 synthetic recall@10 / 0.936 nDCG@10 were restored.

Next implementation sequence

09 — Stratified exhaustive candidate oracle

Build a deterministic oracle over sampled complete window sets: both markets, 63/126/252-bar lookbacks, multiple historical cutoffs, liquid and illiquid stocks, and diverse chart regimes. Compute the exact composite distance for every sampled window and store immutable rankings.

Pass: repeatable digests; zero future/overlap violations; at least 95% top-20 recall measured against exhaustive rankings, not merely a larger coarse pool.

10 — Deterministic multi-view candidate union

Retrieve separate quotas for price correlation/MASS, magnitude/stage development, candle-volatility, volume/shock, and benchmark/relative-strength views. Combine them by deterministic rank fusion and deduplicate before exact reranking. Avoid one weighted proxy that can hide a strong match in one view.

Pass: ≥95% recall on Gate 09 with a bounded pool; no drop below the existing synthetic thresholds.

11 — Exact safe pruning and persisted shards

Add LB_Keogh/LB_Webb bounds for the DTW portion, partitioned representation shards, resumable checkpoints, source fingerprints and code/config hashes. Use parallel readers while preserving deterministic ranking.

Pass: pruned and brute-force rankings are identical on the exhaustive oracle; interrupted jobs resume to the same digest.

12 — Multi-query complete-universe matrix

Run at least 12 locked queries across NSE/NASDAQ, three lookbacks and multiple regimes. Record recall, latency, RSS, candidate saturation, quality coverage and serial/parallel determinism.

Pass: ≥95% top-20 recall for every query; zero failures; NSE ≤120 s and NASDAQ ≤300 s per 252-bar query on this 8-core/31-GiB host; peak RSS ≤1 GiB.

13 — Historical usefulness, kept separate

Only after retrieval passes: perform locked walk-forward outcome stability with delisting/survivorship disclosure, corporate-action checks, regime strata, censored horizons and trial accounting.

Pass: this gate evaluates descriptive stability; it must not be used to retune the representation on the same folds.
