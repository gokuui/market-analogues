WF-03D — Prediction-before-query-outcome ordering

  1. Seal all neighbor links and retrieval certificates with no outcome columns.
  2. Compute or reuse analogue outcomes only when that particular outcome completion timestamp is no later than the query cutoff. Ambiguous and censored states remain visible.
  3. Freeze raw analogue links, method-specific probabilities/quantiles, abstention inputs and all baseline predictions for every query.
  4. Only then compute query outcomes for development and validation 1–3. Verify those folds without changing formulas or thresholds.
  5. Open final 2024–August-2025 query outcomes exactly once only after all earlier artifacts and the final predictions independently verify.

This ordering prevents a query’s realized future from selecting its neighbors, changing a baseline, altering abstention or deciding which prediction gets published.

Acceptance boundary

WF-03 completion proves causal, exact and performant prediction construction. It does not prove usefulness. WF-04 alone measures Brier/log/interval performance and may fail. Even a research pass cannot clear missing membership, delisting, sector and event-cluster limitations or authorize production.
