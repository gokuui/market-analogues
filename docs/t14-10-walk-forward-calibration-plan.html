What cannot be gamed by abstention

Every evaluable query receives a forced-lane score even if novelty, sample size, instability or data quality would hide a user-facing probability. A separate selective lane reports retained coverage and risk-versus-coverage. Abstention therefore cannot manufacture a good proper score by discarding difficult failures.
